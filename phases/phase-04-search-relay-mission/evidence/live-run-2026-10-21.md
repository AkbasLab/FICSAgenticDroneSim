## Repeatability

* **Drone1, flights 2 and 3 at the same instant** (from each record's start, 0.1 s apart, 385 instants): at most 0.20 m apart, median 0.08 m.
* **Drone2, flights 2 and 3 at the same instant** (from each record's start, 0.1 s apart, 385 instants): at most 0.22 m apart, median 0.07 m.
* **Report times, flights 2 and 3:** within tens of milliseconds on every seed but 6. Seed 6: T2 13.3 s against 27.9 s.

## Observations

* **Flight 1's first 2 s are the drones falling into place.** `ensure_vehicles`
  places each drone about 29 m up and waits a fixed 1.5 s. Flight 1's first
  polls caught both still falling, at 17 m, which is why its height error
  (8.9 and 12.9 m), battery figures and times are off: everything there is
  shifted about 2.1 s. Its verdicts depend on none of those seconds.
  `fly_live` now waits for the drones to come to rest (`wait_at_rest`, 2.5 s
  in flights 2 and 3) before observing. Flight 1's record is kept as flown.
* **Flight 3 says dirty only because flight 2's result files were not yet
  committed.** Provenance counts any untracked file. Its `agentic_uav` digest
  is the one flight 2 recorded at a clean `2fd0e32`: the same code.
* **The grounds match Phase 3's.** TAKE_OFF measured 29.245 and 29.282 under
  these two pads in every flight, the values Phase 3 measured.
* **The cruise-height error is CarlaAir rising at the end of the climb and
  at every turn.** In flight 2, the polls more than 1 m above cruise height
  come at three places: the top of the climb over the pad (up to 1.65 m), the
  route's corner (Drone1), and the lane-end turns and just after them. The
  worst, 2.48 m for Drone1 and 2.07 m for Drone2, are at the turns into and
  out of the lanes. The drones also run about 1.3 m past each lane end (x 21.3
  for a lane ending at 20).
* **The maximum off track, about 2.8 m, is the arrival home.** In flights 2
  and 3, Drone1 runs on past its pad to (−2.07, 1.83) and Drone2 past its pad
  to (2.23, −2.11), before returning to land within 0.3 m. Each runs towards
  the other's side, 4 m apart in height; the closest pair was 4.05 m. The
  median off track is 0.1 m.

  *Corrected at the close, 2026-10-21: this bullet first said every such poll
  was near a lane end and that the lane-end overshoot set the 2.8 m maximum.
  Checking the polls point by point showed the climb, the route's corner and
  the arrival home.*
* **Landed heights differ by drone.** Drone2 reads 1.18–1.19 m after landing,
  as Phase 3 measured, and Drone1 0.0 m. Both count as landed under the
  poller's 3 m line (fixed before flight 1, from Phase 3's numbers).
* **Live is slower than the mock and costs more battery, within the
  estimate.** Completion is 37.4–37.5 s against the mock's 31.1 s. Battery
  used, Drone1 / Drone2, in flights 2 and 3:

  | | Drone1 | Drone2 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Live | 3.82–3.83 % | 3.91–3.92 % |
  | Mock | 3.44 % | 3.66 % |
  | Plan's estimate | 4.13 % | 4.40 % |

* **One report shows how much a range boundary matters.** On seed 6, T2 at
  (−3, −15) is detected by Drone1 at 13.3 s in both flights 2 and 3.
  * In flight 2 Drone1 was 19.999 m from the base at that moment, inside the
    20 m range, so T2 was reported at once.
  * In flight 3 it was 20.028 m away, so the report waited for the way home,
    at 27.9 s.

  Three centimetres of live drift moved a report by 14.6 s. The verdict was
  the same; under degraded communications (Phase 10) it might not be.
