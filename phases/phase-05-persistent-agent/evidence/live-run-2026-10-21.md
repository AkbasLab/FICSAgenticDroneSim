## Observations

* **Live is slower than the mock in every phase, by the same amounts each
  flight.** Take-off 9.6 s against 7.5 s, the route 3.6 against 2.5, the
  search 10.8 against 8.0, home 7.3 against 5.5, landing 7.2 against 6.5:
  completion 37.2–37.3 s against 30.0 s. The agent's decisions did not
  change; only when they came.
* **The polled battery reads a little high.** 96.1 % left live against the
  mock's 96.5 %, though live flies longer: below the poller's 3 m line nothing
  counts as airborne, so the climb and descent through it are not charged.
  The readings the agent acted on use the same rule as the judge, so they
  agree with each other; neither is the mock's exact figure.
* **R1 was kept clear by 2.00 m in every flight,** on the route east of it,
  the planned route passing 2.1 m from it (x 0.1 against R1's edge at −2).
* **The lane ends overshoot, as in Phase 4.** x flown −9.43 to about 22.0
  for a lane from −8 to 20, at most 28.4 m from the pad and 14.4 m up
  (cruise 12 m). The planned path stays inside the box Phase 4 flew; the
  flown one runs 1.4–2 m past each lane end, as Phase 4's Drone1 did.
* **Drone1 lands 1.1–1.5 m from its pad,** in all four flights, against
  0.26–0.34 m in Phase 4's flights of the same drone. Its landed height read
  1.15–1.17 m three times and 0.05 m once, as Phase 4 also saw it vary. No
  criterion turns on either: C6 asks for home, landed, above the reserve, and
  both heights are under the 3 m line.
* **The mock twin is the same scenario, task and fault on the mock,** flown
  by the script beside each live flight. It made the same decisions on the
  same events every time.
